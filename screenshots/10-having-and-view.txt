=== 10A: HAVING QUERY (CONTRACTS WITH >= 2 MILESTONES) ===

SQL:
SELECT 
        contract_id, 
        COUNT(*)::int AS milestone_count, 
        SUM(amount) AS total_contract_value
      FROM milestone
      GROUP BY contract_id
      HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2
      ORDER BY contract_id;

OUTPUT:
contract_id | milestone_count | total_contract_value
------------|-----------------|---------------------
1           | 3               | 50000.00            
2           | 3               | 20000.00            

Total rows: 2
